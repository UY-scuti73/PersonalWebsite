Transferable Skills

Leadership

Evidence: AI Hallucination Research + Learning Assistant

For my senior capstone project, I served as the project manager—keeping the team aligned on deadlines, coordinating tasks, and communicating progress with supervisors while implementation moved forward. In addition, as a Learning Assistant, I led discussion groups with peers to support effective learning strategies.

Teamwork

Evidence: Various Activities

I have learned how to collaborate effectively through many of my activities, both in school and in extracurricular settings. Group projects like the AI Hallucination Research capstone and Yapp were built by working closely with teammates and combining our individual strengths. Working as an administrative assistant also taught me how to coordinate with coworkers and handle difficult situations professionally.

Communication

Evidence: Learning Assistant + Mock Interview

As a Learning Assistant, I learned to explain difficult concepts clearly and adapt explanations to different learning styles. The mock interview further strengthened my ability to communicate in a structured, professional way.

Agile Development

Evidence: AI Hallucination Research + Yapp

Both the AI Hallucination Research project and Yapp were developed in teams using Agile and Scrum workflows, with work organized into sprints and progress presented throughout the semester. This experience gave me practice working in a development cycle that closely matches how software is built in industry.

Ownership and Reliability

Evidence: Mod Development (Live users + rolling updates)

Maintaining mods long-term built strong follow-through: handling bug reports, prioritizing feature requests, shipping updates, and protecting stability and performance for real users.

Working Under Constraints

Evidence: Godot Game Jam

I worked within a fixed deadline, iterated quickly, and helped polish the final result—balancing scope, quality, and speed.

Product Deployment and User Empathy

Evidence: Community feedback → mod updates

Managing user feedback taught me how to prioritize what matters most, ship improvements that users notice immediately, and focus on usability and performance. Through these updates, I also learned how to deploy deliverables responsibly and maintain clear release notes, changelogs, and GitHub-based workflows.

Miscellaneous Skills

Astrophysics

I have a strong background in astronomy and astrophysics, and this has been a long-term passion of mine. At the University of Florida, I developed that interest into formal coursework and a deeper technical foundation. This knowledge can support roles connected to scientific research or science-driven problem solving, since astronomy draws on multiple disciplines. Skills from advanced astronomy, along with core physics, chemistry, and geology concepts, translate well to a wide range of analytical tasks.

Geography

I have learned a lot about international culture and geography through GeoGuessr, a game where you identify locations from panoramic images. Over time, this has helped me build strong world knowledge and sharper pattern recognition. These skills help with practical context around travel and global awareness, and they reinforce problem-solving by making careful, evidence-based guesses from limited information.

Personal Finance

I have developed an interest in personal finance and hope to eventually extend this knowledge to larger, macro-scale questions. Learning about budgeting, debt, and long-term planning has improved how I analyze tradeoffs and make practical recommendations.
